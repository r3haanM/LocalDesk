    Intercept

    Every job runs inside the local tab. Content streams are captured in memory before a single byte could ever leave the device.

  2. 02

    Purge

    Redacted runs are excised from the content stream itself — the data is gone, not hidden behind a rectangle someone can peel off.

    Verify

    A second pass re-parses the output and confirms zero residual matches, sealing the result with a SHA-256 signature.
