security & vault

Redaction that
actually deletes.

Most tools paint a black box over your secrets and call it a day — lift the box and the text is still underneath. Local Desk reaches into the PDF content stream, purges the literal and hex-encoded strings, re-parses its own export and verifies they are gone.

INTERNAL MEMO — CONFIDENTIAL

Payroll exception for R-4402-MK approved by J. Alvarez on 11/03. Direct deposit to account 8837-**-4410 remains active. Vault key rotation scheduled for Q1.

Shareholder briefing packet follows on page 4. Legal sign-off attached.

// try it — this demo runs in your browser, just like the real engine

  1. 01

    Intercept

    Every removed or edited string is tracked from the canvas down to its raw content-stream operator — Tj, TJ, literal and hex forms.

  2. 02

    Purge

    The underlying PDF stream is rewritten with the strings excised — not covered, not clipped. Gone from the bytes, not just the pixels.

  3. 03

    Verify

    The exported file is re-parsed and searched for every purged string. The verification report is shown to you before you save.
